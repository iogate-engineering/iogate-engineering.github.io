Privacy notice

iogate respects your privacy. This notice explains what happens to personal information you provide through this website and how to contact us about it.

Who is responsible for your information?

iogate is responsible for the personal information described in this notice. For privacy questions or requests, email info@iogate.co.uk.

What we collect

If you use the contact form, we collect your name, email address, optional company name, and anything you include in your message. Please do not send sensitive personal information through the form.

Our hosting and font-delivery providers may also receive technical information needed to deliver the website, such as your IP address, browser type, requested page, and access time.

Why we use it

We use enquiry information to understand your request, reply to you, and discuss relevant services. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. You do not have to provide this information, but without a name, email address, and message we cannot respond through the form.

We do not use the enquiry form to add you to a marketing list, and this website does not use analytics, advertising, or automated decision-making.

Who receives it

Contact-form submissions are processed by Formspree, which accepts the submission and sends it to iogate. Formspree states that it processes information in the United States and other countries and takes steps to protect personal information transferred outside Europe. Website hosting and font-delivery providers process the limited technical information needed to deliver their services.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose it if required by law or where reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal rights.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only for as long as it is needed to respond, manage any resulting business relationship, meet legal or accounting obligations, or resolve a dispute. We periodically review information that is no longer active and delete it when there is no continuing need to retain it.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to our use of your personal information, and to receive a portable copy. To make a request, email info@iogate.co.uk. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office through the ICO complaint process.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the website, our suppliers, or our data practices change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
