Who we work with

We work primarily with startups and growing organisations that need to build or mature their cloud environments with confidence. We typically engage where internal teams are strong on product, but need experienced cloud leadership to design and implement the underlying platform properly.

  • Early-stage startups Launching or stabilising production workloads and avoiding infrastructure that becomes a liability as the product scales.
  • Growing technology teams Improving security, structure and automation as you move beyond an initial cloud setup.
  • Smaller organisations modernising infrastructure Increasing resilience, cost control and operational clarity without enterprise complexity.

What we do

  • Cloud Foundation Setup Secure, production-ready cloud deployments designed for growth. IAM, guardrails, IaC and cost visibility — implemented properly from the start.
  • Platform & DevOps Acceleration CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure automation that reduce manual overhead and improve release confidence — helping teams move faster without increasing risk.
  • Architecture Review & Stabilisation A focused review of your cloud environment to identify security risks and inefficiencies — with a clear, pragmatic roadmap for improvement.

IOGate was founded to provide senior-level cloud expertise without enterprise complexity. Our background spans secure networks, high-availability systems and delivery in high-trust and regulated environments — bringing structure, clarity and discipline to fast-moving organisations.
